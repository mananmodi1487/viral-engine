# Broadcaster brief prompt

Basis: popularity tracks the largest single broadcast more than chains (Goel, Watts & Goldstein 2012/2016). Optimising average share rate is optimising the wrong statistic.

Produce one primary broadcaster and up to two alternates. For each:
- target: a role plus where to find them. Never a fabricated individual.
- kind: maintainer | newsletter | community-moderator | practitioner-with-audience | aggregator
- whereToFind: a concrete surface (topic search, directory, forum), not "social media"
- whyTheyCare
- whatMakesThemLookGood
- assetToSend
- messageTemplate: ready to paste, no invented specifics about the person
- whatNotToSay (at least one)
- likelyAmplifier: the durable surface that keeps sending traffic
- failureCondition

Reject "post on social media", "find influencers", and any advice that names no specific kind of person. If you cannot name a broadcaster, say so — that is a finding.
