# Launch asset prompt

Produce assets for: Show HN, X thread, LinkedIn, Product Hunt, landing hero, email subjects, chat announcement, and one paste-ready broadcaster line.

Rules:
- Include the channel's real budget where known (Show HN one paragraph; PH tagline under 60 chars; X first post under 240 chars).
- No invented numbers. Use `[your real number]`.
- No countdown timers, no manufactured urgency, no engagement bait.
- Each asset must contain one checkable claim or an honest placeholder.
- The paste-ready line is the most important asset: one sentence a broadcaster could repeat from memory.

Also produce 3-5 kill criteria, each with an observable signal and a time horizon, written before launch so they cannot be rationalised afterwards.
