# Positioning prompt

1. Name the crowded category the document currently competes in, and why it is crowded.
2. Propose three adjacent categories with the same underlying benefit but a different buyer, budget, or emotion. For each: why less crowded, tension 0-5, what could break the claim.
3. Pick the one where the object is most unusual and most needed. Usually the highest tension with survivable credibility.
4. Write: "It's not <current>. It's <new> — for <specific person> who <specific situation>."
5. State the proof required: what must be shown for the frame to be true rather than a costume.
6. State the failure condition: what would tell you the frame is not landing, and when you would know.

Reject any frame that needs a paragraph to explain, that has no budget or attention, or that the product cannot survive. Show the rejected frames and why — a plan without rejected options is a preference.
