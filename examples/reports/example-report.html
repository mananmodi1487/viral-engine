7. Pre-launch checklist

  • blocker Replace the one-line description with the proposed positioning sentence.
    The description is the only text most people will ever read; if it names the wrong category, nothing downstream recovers.
  • blocker Produce one screenshot-ready artifact from real data (no personal information).
    A share with no artifact requires the sharer to write an explanation. That is the friction that kills most sharing.
  • blocker Write down the failure condition before launch, not after.
    A plan without a kill criterion cannot be evaluated; it can only be rationalised.
  • warning Send the asset to one named broadcaster and ask for nothing.
    Broadcast drives popularity more than chains do (Goel et al.), but the first message must not ask for a favour.
  • blocker Re-run the honesty scan on the final copy before publishing.
    Copy drifts during editing; a late "10x" is how a clean draft becomes a liability.
  • warning Pick one channel and one asset. Write the other channels into SUMMARY.md as "later".
    Simultaneous multi-channel launches split attention and make every channel look like a failure.
  • info Read the first 20 replies manually and note which sentence gets quoted back.
    The quoted sentence is the next post; the reply sentiment tells you whether the frame landed or backfired.

8. Kill criteria

Written down before launch, so they cannot be rationalised afterwards.

  • The output reads as generic advice rather than a diagnosis of this specific document.
    Observable: A reader cannot tell which project the report was about without being told. · Horizon: Immediately, on your own read-through
  • A reply corrects a factual claim in the launch copy.
    Observable: Any comment disputing a number, a testimonial, or a capability. · Horizon: First 24 hours
  • People engage with the frame but nobody forwards it.
    Observable: Replies and likes present, shares and DMs absent after 7 days. · Horizon: 7 days
  • Installs happen but nobody returns.
    Observable: 7-day return rate below 30%. · Horizon: 30 days
