# 14 — When not to launch

**20 minutes.** The module most launch advice will never give you.

## The premise

Launching is irreversible in one specific way: it spends your one chance to be new. A reader who
sees a weak version first does not come back to see the strong version. They have already placed
you in a category and moved on.

So "not yet" is often the highest-value answer, and a tool that cannot say it is a tool that
pushes you off a cliff to feel useful.

## The four conditions

**1. A reader cannot tell what it is.**
If five strangers cannot say what it does after one read, launching spends the chance. Test
first (module 09). This is the most common and the most fixable.

**2. The first five minutes do not work.**
Dead link, empty repository, install that fails on a clean machine, signup that requires an
invite. The forwarder will not debug this for you.

**3. The claims cannot survive checking.**
Any blocker in the honesty audit. A launch with an inflated claim is not a launch, it is a loan
against your credibility, and the interest is charged publicly.

**4. The thing is not the thing you are describing.**
If your description names a job the artifact does not do, the people who arrive will leave, and
they will be right to. Ship the description you have, or build the artifact the description
names. Not both, not halfway.

## What is *not* a reason to delay

These are fear wearing the costume of diligence:

- The design is not finished. (Nobody has ever noticed a design detail you were embarrassed by.)
- There are not enough features. (Nobody has ever forwarded something for its feature count.)
- A competitor launched. (Their launch does not change whether yours is ready.)
- It is not the perfect week. (There is no perfect week; there is only the week you pick.)
- It is not perfect. (This one can coexist with all four conditions above; that is the skill.)

## The decision procedure

```
Is any of the four conditions true?
  → yes: do not launch. Fix the specific one. Set a date to re-check.
  → no: launch. Today. The remaining work is not preparation, it is avoidance.
```

Note the asymmetry. Three of the four conditions are cheap to fix and expensive to skip. The
fear-based reasons cost nothing to ignore and feel expensive to. That contrast is why people
delay for months on decoration and launch on a broken install link.

## "Do not launch yet" is a real output

The engine can return `do_not_launch_yet: true` with a reason. It is not an error, and it is not
a failure of the tool. If your report says it, read the reason rather than the score. Then either
fix the thing or decide, explicitly, that the reason does not apply — and write down why.

## What this module cannot tell you

- Whether your category is in a bad moment. That is market information nobody has.
- Whether the work is good. This academy is about whether it is *legible*, which is a different
  and more tractable question.
