# 🚀 48-Hour Launch Checklist

Print it. Cross things off. Everything here is doable by one person with no budget.

The rule that governs the whole thing: **week 3 kills more projects than launch day ever did.** This checklist is the start, not the finish.

---

## T-minus: before you say anything

- [ ] The thing works from a clean start — tested 5 times, not once
- [ ] A stranger completed it without help (if they needed help, fix the thing, not the copy)
- [ ] Every claim in the description is true or marked `[placeholder]`
- [ ] There's one link, and it goes where you think it goes
- [ ] Social preview image set — this is the card on every share
- [ ] One action is asked for. Not two.
- [ ] You have written down: **this fails if ____________**

**Gate:** don't launch until 10 strangers have tried it and 5 reported something useful. Friends compliment. Strangers report.

---

## Hour 0–2 · The frame

- [ ] Replace the description with the new category. Same product, new frame. **No feature work.**
- [ ] Rewrite the first line only. Everything else can stay broken.
- [ ] Read line one out loud to one human. Ask "what do you think this is about?"

---

## Hour 2–4 · The artifact

- [ ] Build the screenshot-ready output — the ugly version, today
- [ ] Check it's legible at 400px wide (that's how most people will see it)
- [ ] Remove any personal data from it
- [ ] Make sure it needs no caption to be understood

---

## Hour 4–6 · The first 20

- [ ] List 20 real people/communities who have this exact problem
- [ ] Message them **individually** — no bcc, no blast
- [ ] Ask for *their result*, not their opinion
- [ ] Do not ask for a star, a share, or a testimonial yet

---

## Day 1 evening · The evidence

- [ ] Collect the 3 best real outputs
- [ ] Ask permission before quoting anyone
- [ ] Add them to the repo — this is the first time you're allowed to claim results
- [ ] Screenshot only. No invented quotes, ever.

---

## Day 2 morning · One channel, one post

- [ ] Pick **one** channel whose rules allow it. Read the rules first.
- [ ] Post the story, not the ad: "here's what I built and what it told me"
- [ ] Title is plain and non-promotional
- [ ] You will be present in the comments for 6 hours. Block the time.

---

## Day 2 afternoon · The work nobody does

- [ ] Answer every comment personally, including the hostile ones
- [ ] Ask questions instead of defending the frame
- [ ] Note the exact sentence people quote back to you — that's your next post
- [ ] Log: views, shares, signups, and the first 20 replies' sentiment

---

## Day 2 evening · Decide

- [ ] Strong hook, no shares → the share trigger is missing (structure, not copy)
- [ ] Shares, no conversion → wrong audience or weak close
- [ ] Good numbers, hostile replies → the claim overreached. Fix honesty before reach.
- [ ] Nothing at all → the bottleneck lever was misdiagnosed. Go back to the diagnosis.

**Change one variable.** Two changes and you've learned nothing.

---

## The week after (this is where it's actually won)

- [ ] Ship one new example/update. Then another, next week.
- [ ] Submit to every directory that accepts it — low effort, long tail
- [ ] Write a short "why we cut this" post. Rejections are more interesting than results.
- [ ] Invite 3 contributors by name
- [ ] Build the durable channel that survives if the spike doesn't (search, newsletter, docs, integration)

---

## Scorecard

| Metric | Working | Action if low |
|---|---|---|
| Hook rate (3-sec view-through) | > 45% | Rewrite line one only |
| Share ratio (shares ÷ views) | > 1.5% text / > 0.25 artifact sends | Test the artifact before the copy |
| Reply quality (% describing their own situation) | > 50% | You triggered debate, not recognition |
| 7-day retention | > 30% | You have a spike, not a product. Stop promoting. |

---

## Things that will make this fail

1. Generic output. Advice-shaped text is not screenshot-shaped.
2. Overpromising. "Make anything go viral" invites the reply "nothing can."
3. One fabricated result. Then the replies become the story.
4. Burning your one real launch attempt on an unpolished version.
5. Stopping at launch.
