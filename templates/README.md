# 📋 Templates

Copy-paste frameworks. No theory, no signup, no email gate. Open a file, fill the blanks, ship.

| Template | Use it when | Time |
|---|---|---|
| [**hook-factory.md**](hook-factory.md) | You have something to say and no first line | 20 min |
| [**positioning-worksheet.md**](positioning-worksheet.md) | Nobody cares about your thing and you suspect it's the category | 30 min |
| [**share-structure-canvas.md**](share-structure-canvas.md) | You're about to publish and want to check it's shareable | 15 min |
| [**launch-checklist.md**](launch-checklist.md) | You're launching in 48 hours | 48 hrs |

---

## How to use these properly

**Do them in this order.** Positioning before hooks before structure before launch. Getting the hook right on the wrong category is the most common wasted week in marketing.

```
Positioning  →  Hooks  →  Share structure  →  Launch
(what game)     (first line)   (why share)        (48 hrs)
```

**Fill them in by hand if you can.** Typing into blanks produces worse answers than writing them, and it's slower, which is the point.

**The blanks that matter most** are the ones about *other people*: the recipient, the broadcaster, the fan's forwarding sentence. Those are the ones people skip, and they're the ones the research says actually drive spread.

---

## The rules that apply to all four

- **No invented numbers.** If you don't have the stat, write `[your real number]` and go measure it. A fabricated figure is the fastest way to lose the argument in the replies.
- **One action per artifact.** Two calls to action means zero.
- **Name a real person or community, not a persona.** "Marketing managers" is not an audience. A specific Slack group is.
- **Write the failure condition.** Every plan gets one line: *this fails if ___*. It tells you what to watch.
- **Read the platform's rules before you post.** Self-promo bans are real.

---

## What these are not

They are not a scoring system that guarantees an outcome. There is no such system — see [the research cheat sheet](../research/virality-patterns.md#5-what-the-research-does-not-say) for the specific reason why.

They are a way to stop shipping things that were never shareable in the first place. That's a lower bar and a much more useful one.
