WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE SPECIFICALLY IMPORTANT CASE

  You may implement the Open Diffusion Spec in a closed-source product, in a
  competing product, in a product that criticises this one, or in a product built
  by someone who has never heard of this repository. You may fork the spec, rename
  it, and change it. No permission, fee, notice, or attribution is required.

  You may not describe your implementation as "ODS 1.0.0 conformant" if it
  disagrees with protocol/conformance.md. That is a factual claim about behaviour,
  not a licensed trademark, and the checks are public.

WHY CC0 AND NOT CC BY

  The spec is the part of this project that must be free of every friction,
  including the friction of remembering where it came from. A protocol that asks
  for attribution on every derivative document is a protocol that asks a lawyer's
  question of a person trying to describe their own work.
