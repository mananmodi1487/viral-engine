# Broadcaster patterns

Who actually passes work on, and under what conditions. The claim this directory tests:
diffusion is mostly broadcast, not chains, and the broadcaster is a specific person with a
specific reason to forward, not "the community".

## What the published research supports

| Finding | Source | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Most diffusion attributed to person-to-person spread is better explained by broadcast from a single source | Goel, Watts & Goldstein (2012), *Prediction and Inference of Diffusion* | Stop optimising for cascades. Optimise for one specific person deciding to post it. |
| Large cascades are rare and their size is driven by early structural luck as much as content | Goel, Watts & Goldstein (2012) | A cascade that did not happen is weak evidence that the work was bad. |
| Content that produces high-arousal emotion (awe, anger, anxiety) travels further than low-arousal content (sadness) | Berger & Milkman (2012), *JMR* | Archetype chosen for effect, not for honesty, is how manipulation enters. See `../ethics-incidents/`. |
| Only ~7% of word of mouth happens online | Berger, *Contagious* (2013) | Online channels are not the whole picture; a tool that only sees online will always be surprised. |

**These are published findings, not our measurements.** They are cited so a reader can check
them. We have not replicated any of them, and the lexicon-based signals in this codebase are
a crude operationalisation of the first two, not a test of them.

## Patterns we have observed (and the label on each)

| Pattern | Observation | Label | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| The practitioner, not the influencer | The forwards that mattered came from people who do the job the tool addresses | self-reported, n=3 | low |
| The reply, not the post | A public reply that says "here is the exact problem" was forwarded more than the announcement | anecdotal | very low |
| The newsletter curator | One person with a small list, who explains why, outperformed a broad mention | self-reported | low |
| The internal forward | Most real usage arrived via a forward inside one organisation, invisible to us | unclearly measured | very low |

**Read the label column.** Every one of these is a small-n, first-person observation. Not
one of them would survive a peer reviewer, and none should be quoted as a rate. They are
here because a shared vocabulary for hypotheses is useful even when the hypotheses are weak.

## What would falsify the whole directory

> If a controlled attempt shows that a broad, unfocused mention produces more **consented,
> traceable forwards** than a targeted broadcaster brief, this directory's premise is wrong.

Recorded here so the premise can lose.

## Consent

Every record in this directory describes behaviour in public channels, at the level of
roles and patterns. **We do not name individuals, we do not profile them, and no record
here is derived from scraping or from private data.** Where a specific person was involved
and could be identified, either consent was obtained or the record is redacted.
