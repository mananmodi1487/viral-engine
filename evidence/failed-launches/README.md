# Failed launches

Launches that went out and did nothing. The most valuable directory in this repository, and
the one that will stay the emptiest, because nobody submits to it voluntarily.

## Why it is valuable

Every other collection of launch advice is filtered by survival. The advice you can read
publicly is written by the people who won, describing what they did, and it systematically
omits the people who did the same thing and lost.

If this directory ever contains twenty well-labelled failures using the same methods as the
wins in `../wins/`, it will be worth more than every "how I got X stars" post combined.

## The one requirement

**Nobody is named without consent.** Not the founder, not the colleague who wrote the copy,
not the community that ignored it. Use the organisation's own public launch if you are the
organisation; otherwise anonymise to the shape.

`private-redacted` is a legitimate label and not a lesser one.

## Minimum viable record

```
- What went out (channel, date, audience size if known)
- The sentence that was supposed to carry it
- What happened, in numbers, or "no numbers available"
- What it was expected to do
- The confound you cannot rule out
- What you would test next time
```

Six lines. If you have a failed launch and six lines of honesty, that is enough. The
directory will take shape slowly and that is fine.

## What we will not do

- We will not accept a failure record that mocks its author. The tone of this repository is
  that failure is the default state of launching, not a personal defect.
- We will not accept a "failed launch" that is really a humble-brag with a sad title.
- We will not aggregate these into a success rate. See `../README.md`.
